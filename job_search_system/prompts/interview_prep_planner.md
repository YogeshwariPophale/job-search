# Interview Prep Planner Prompt

Use this to build a role-specific 2-month interview plan.

```text
Build my Interview Prep Planner using my job_search_system.

Use:
- job_search_system/profile/candidate_profile.md
- job_search_system/profile/job_search_profile.md
- job_search_system/profile/preferences.yml
- job_search_system/templates/interview_prep_intake.md

Interview input:
- Company: [company]
- Role: [role]
- Stage: [just applied / recruiter screen / first round / technical / final / unknown]
- Job description: [paste if available]
- Interview date or target date: [date if known]
- Hours per week available: [hours]
- Known gaps or concerns: [optional]

Build a plan for 8 weeks, compressed if the interview is sooner.

Phases:
1. Weeks 1-2 - Foundation
   - Understand the role
   - Research company, product, customers, data stack signals, and business model
   - Build an 8-10 story STAR bank
2. Weeks 3-4 - Core Prep
   - Behavioral answers
   - Role-specific technical refresh
   - Company-specific answers
   - Start mock interviews
3. Weeks 5-6 - Deep Practice
   - SQL/Python/statistics/case or analytics exercises as relevant
   - Timed STAR stories
   - Interviewer research
   - Questions to ask
4. Weeks 7-8 - Simulation
   - Full mock interviews
   - Weak area repair
   - Panel prep
   - Day-of logistics and follow-up templates

Output:
- Week-by-week plan
- Daily focus areas calibrated to available hours
- Role-specific question bank
- 8-10 STAR story prompts mapped to Yogeshwari's real experience
- The 3 things most likely to decide whether she gets the offer
- The most common mistake for this role type
- Follow-up email template

Rules:
- Browse for current company/interview information when the company is named.
- Keep examples grounded in Yogeshwari's actual profile.
- Do not create fake project details or claim unsupported tools.
```
