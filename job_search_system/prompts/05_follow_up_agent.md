# Codex Prompt - Follow-Up Agent

Use this after an application is submitted or a recruiter contact is created.

## Timing

- Application follow-up: 5-7 business days after applying.
- Recruiter connection follow-up: 3-5 business days after message.
- Interview thank-you: within 24 hours.

## Follow-Up Message

Hi {{name}},

I hope you are doing well. I recently applied for the {{role}} position at {{company}} and wanted to briefly reiterate my interest. My background combines SQL, Python, data quality workflows, KPI reporting, and manufacturing analytics experience across Bajaj Auto and Rolls-Royce Power Systems. I would be excited to contribute that mix of analytics and operations experience to your team.

Thank you for your time, and I would be happy to share any additional information.

Best,
Yogeshwari Pophale
